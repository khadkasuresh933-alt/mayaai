How to install

  1. Open the download link on your Android phone and download the APK.
  2. Tap the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Maya AI, sign in, and add your Gemini key in Settings > Connections.
  5. For full phone control, turn on Maya AI Control in Accessibility settings. This is optional.

Maya AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Information we collect

  • Name and email address, when you sign in
  • Chat messages and voice conversations
  • Memory notes you choose to save
  • Profile photo, stored only on your phone
  • Images and PDF files you choose to upload
  • Contacts, used only to find a person by name when you ask to call or message. They are not stored or sent to our servers.
  • Screen and camera images, only while you turn on Screen Share or Live Camera
  • Pro subscription status
  • Device information needed for the app to work

How we use your information

  • To provide AI chat and voice replies
  • To save chat history on your device
  • To enable voice, camera, image, PDF and phone-control features
  • To improve app performance and security

Permissions

Internet, Microphone, Camera, Contacts, Phone and Notifications. Each is used only for the feature it belongs to.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (sign-in, Pro status, and our chat server)
  • Google Gemini, Groq and OpenAI (to generate AI replies)

Messages, images, PDFs and voice you send for a reply are processed by these services under their own privacy policies.

Data security

We use reasonable security measures. No internet service can guarantee 100% security.

Delete your account

Children's privacy

Delete your Maya AI account

In the app

  1. Sign out, then sign in again (Android requires a recent sign-in to delete).
  2. Open Settings > Privacy & about > Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

Send an email from the address you signed up with to khadkasuresh933@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Maya AI account".

What is deleted

Your sign-in account and Pro status are deleted within 30 days. Chats, memory and your profile photo are stored only on your phone and are removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.
