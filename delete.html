Delete your Maya AI account

App: Maya AI (Android) · Developer: Suresh Khadka

In the app

  1. Sign out, then sign in again (Android requires a recent sign-in to delete).
  2. Open Settings > Privacy & about > Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

Send an email from the address you signed up with to khadkasuresh933@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Maya AI account". We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email) and Pro status are deleted.
  • Chats, memory, API keys and your profile photo are stored only on your phone. They are removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.
